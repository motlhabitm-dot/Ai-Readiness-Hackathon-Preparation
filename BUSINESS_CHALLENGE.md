**Problem Statement**

Corporate training teams lack a secure, automated, Ai assisted project management dashboard to collaborate safely, automate workflows and deliver a governance-aligned solution

**Affected User**

- Training Team
- IT administrators
- External Stakeholders
- Senior mangement

**Impact Evidence**

Solving this problem can help the training team:

Reduce time spent on manual project administration.
Improve visibility of project progress and risks.
Enable safer and more controlled use of AI.
Automate repetitive workflows.
Support better, faster decision-making.
Provide a governance-aligned foundation for future AI adoption.
